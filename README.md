# 💰 Creators Hub

**The Social Platform Where Creators Post Content & Earn Money**

A modern, free platform where anyone can create posts, build an audience, and earn money through tips. Built for creators, by creators.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💬 Create & Share
- 📝 Post text, images, and ideas
- 📂 Organize by categories (Tech, Design, Business, Lifestyle, Education, Art)
- 🖼️ Add images to your posts
- 🔍 Search posts by content or creator

### 💰 Earn Money
- 💵 Get tips from your audience ($1, $5, $10, $25)
- 📊 Track your earnings in real-time
- 🏆 See top earners and trending creators
- 📈 Monitor your engagement (likes, comments)

### 👥 Build Your Community
- 👤 Create your creator profile
- 🤝 Follow other creators
- 👥 Discover suggested creators
- 💌 Send direct messages
- 💬 Comment on posts

### 📊 Analytics
- 📈 Track your total earnings
- ❤️ Monitor total likes received
- 📝 See how many posts you've created
- 👥 Check your followers count

## 💡 How It Works

### For Creators:
1. **Create** - Write posts, add images, pick a category
2. **Share** - Share your content with the community
3. **Engage** - Get likes and comments from other creators
4. **Earn** - Receive tips from supporters
5. **Grow** - Build your audience and increase earnings

### For Supporters:
1. **Discover** - Find content by category or search
2. **Engage** - Like posts and leave comments
3. **Support** - Tip creators you love ($1-$25)
4. **Connect** - Follow creators and message them

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is this really free?**

**Q: What happens to my data?**
